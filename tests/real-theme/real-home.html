Dora Canal Court | Vacation Rentals in Tavares, Florida

  • ABOUT US

Family owned and run since 1971, Dora Canal Court offers cottage rentals in a tranquil, waterfront community on the scenic Dora Canal in Tavares, Florida. Our newly remodeled and fully furnished cottages provide a comfortable, low-maintenance retreat between adventures.

With direct boat access to the Harris Chain of Lakes, you can easily explore waterfront restaurants, golf courses, live entertainment, and unique boutiques in Tavares and Mount Dora. Our area’s distinct natural beauty offers numerous outdoor activities, including fishing tournaments, freshwater springs, paddleboarding & kayaking, wildlife preserves, scenic boat tours, and even wild manatees. Additionally, Mount Dora was named by USA Today as the third best small town food scene in the United States.

Dora Canal Court is ideally situated for discovering the best of Central Florida. We’re located between the sunny beaches of both Florida coasts, the festivals and live entertainment in The Villages, and the world-famous theme parks of Orlando.

See why Southern Living named us one of the “best places to stay” in Tavares!

Can I dock and charge my boat?

Yes, we have several boat slips directly on the Dora Canal, which provides guests direct boat access to the entire Harris Chain of Lakes. We've recently upgraded some amenities tailored for our boating and fishing guests.

This includes installing several boat slips, a boat charging station, rebuilding the seawall, and adding a fish cleaning station. To park boats on our boat slips, just launch your boat down the street at Summerall Park.

How close is downtown?

We are less than half a mile from downtown Tavares, known as "America's Seaplane City." Guests can enjoy the nearby waterfront restaurants, seaplane base, live entertainment, unique boutiques, and more. Despite being near the city center, our property is tucked away within the natural beauty of the Dora Canal.

Are pets allowed?

We love animals! However, to keep our rentals in top condition, we only allow a pre-approved dog in one designated cottage — Cottage 34: Coconut Cottage. If you'd like to bring your dog along, please reach out to us for pre-approval before finalizing your reservation.

Cottage 34 accepts one dog up to 20 lbs, and the pet fee is $50 per night.

How many guests can stay?

Every cottage includes 2 guests in the nightly rate, with a queen bed. Six of our cottages — 22, 23, 31, 32, 35, and 36 — can sleep up to 4: the 3rd and 4th guests stay on the pull-out couch for $50 per night each, and we'll provide the extra bedding on request. Cottages 33 and 34 are our two studios and sleep 2 guests only.

Do I have to be 55+?

No, you do not have to be 55+ years old to book any of our rental cottages.

The 55+ age restriction applies only to our residential mobile-home tenants, and never to guests staying in the vacation cottages. That mixed-use setup is what gives our guests a quieter, more relaxing stay than a standard hotel or vacation rental.

What's Dora Canal Court's history?

Dora Canal Court has been family owned and run since 1971. As part of the Tavares community for over 50 years, we are proud to be the third-generation to own and operate carrying forward a family legacy that began with my grandparents.

After they developed dementia, we made a commitment to "rebuild from below the ground up" to preserve the community atmosphere they built. Since then, we have completely reimagined the guest experience, including:

⚓︎ Fully Renovated Cottages: All 8 vacation rentals have been stripped to the studs and refurnished with modern comforts.

⚓︎ Enhanced Waterfront Amenities: We’ve added dedicated boat slips and facilities specifically tailored to boaters and fishers.

⚓︎ New Community Spaces: Guests now enjoy our brand-new community clubhouse and outdoor lounge area, which was designed to create the same relaxing, friendly environment our grandparents envisioned over 50 years ago.
